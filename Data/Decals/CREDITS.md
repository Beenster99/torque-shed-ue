# Decal library credits

Every built-in decal (stripes, shapes and tattoo-flash artwork in `shapes/` and `art/`) is drawn by
script from plain geometry in `Tools/decals/` (shapes.py, flash.py). No outside pictures, brands,
makers' names or logos are used. Remake with `python3 Tools/decals/make_decals.py`.
