Naked rear-end rules

Every bike in the trial grid that lays out as a naked bike, {{TOTAL}} in all, built twice: on the left exactly as the game builds it today, on the right by the first placement rules for the naked type. Each picture has a side view, a top view and a close-up of the rear end.

These rules only cover the rear end so far: the seat, the tail, the subframe under them and what hangs off them. The engine, tank, fairing and chain line are untouched. Nothing here goes into the game; the game's own code is not changed.

What the naked rules ask for

Rule 1The tail joins the seatThe tail starts where the seat ends, its top flush with the seat's top, instead of starting under the seat or leaving a step.
Rule 2Tail angle and length by typeThe tail runs 10° above the seat's line and its tip ends 28 cm behind the rear axle, as on the MT-09. A tail is stretched or shortened by at most 15%. One that would need more than 130% or less than 75% of its length keeps its own shape, is only joined to the seat, and is marked as not suiting.
Rule 3The subframe carries the tailThe subframe rails run on under the tail to near its tip with a loop across, instead of stopping 30 cm short and leaving the tail hanging.
Rule 4The under-tray closes the tailThe tray lies along the tail's lower edge, and the number plate, its hanger and the lamp hang from the tray's end.
Rule 5Pillion pegs bolt to the frameThe pillion peg hangers bend in to meet the nearest subframe rail instead of stopping in mid-air.
